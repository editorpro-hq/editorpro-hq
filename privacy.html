Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2025

Introduction

EditorPro ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how EditorPro handles information when you use our iOS video editing application.

In short: EditorPro collects minimal, anonymous usage data to improve the app. We never collect personal information or access your videos. Your content remains completely private on your device.

Data Collection

What We Collect

EditorPro may collect anonymous usage analytics to help us improve the app, understand which features are used, and identify bugs. This includes:

  • App usage events - Which features you use (e.g., "applied transition", "exported video")
  • Device information - Device model, iOS version, app version
  • Performance data - App crashes, errors, and performance metrics
  • Session data - How long you use the app, session timestamps

What We Do NOT Collect

EditorPro does NOT collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Your videos or photos
  • Video content, thumbnails, or any media files
  • Project names or editing details
  • Precise location data
  • Contact lists or calendar data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Any information that can identify you personally

All analytics data is anonymous and aggregated. We cannot identify individual users from this data. Your videos and creative work remain completely private on your device.

Photo Library Access

EditorPro requires access to your photo library to provide video editing functionality. This access is used exclusively for:

  • Importing videos - When you choose to import a video for editing
  • Saving edited videos - When you export and save your finished projects
  • Generating previews - To display thumbnails and waveforms in the editing interface

Important:

  • We only access photos/videos when you explicitly select them
  • We never scan, analyze, or access your library without your action
  • No photos or videos are uploaded to any server
  • You can revoke this permission anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy → Photos

Local Data Storage

EditorPro stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Video Projects - Your editing projects and timeline configurations
  • Cache Files - Temporary thumbnails and waveforms for performance
  • App Preferences - Your settings and preferences (stored in iOS UserDefaults)

All data remains exclusively on your device. Nothing is synchronized to the cloud or transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Services

Analytics Provider

EditorPro uses PostHog, a privacy-focused analytics platform, to collect anonymous usage data. PostHog helps us:

  • Understand which features are most used
  • Identify and fix bugs and crashes
  • Improve app performance
  • Make data-driven decisions about new features

PostHog collects only the anonymous data listed in the "Data Collection" section above. For more information about PostHog's privacy practices, visit: https://posthog.com/privacy

What We Do NOT Use

EditorPro does NOT use:

  • Advertising networks or ad tracking
  • Cloud storage services for your videos
  • Social media integrations that collect your data
  • Third-party advertising identifiers
  • Data brokers or data selling services

We will never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

How We Use Your Data

The anonymous analytics data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Improve the app - Understand usage patterns and prioritize features
  • Fix bugs - Identify crashes and errors to make the app more stable
  • Optimize performance - Improve speed and efficiency
  • Develop new features - Make informed decisions about what to build next

We never use your data for:

  • Advertising or marketing to you
  • Selling or sharing with third parties
  • Building user profiles for commercial purposes
  • Tracking you across other apps or websites

Network Connectivity

EditorPro requires minimal network connectivity for:

  • Analytics transmission - Sending anonymous usage data to PostHog servers (when online)

All video editing functions work completely offline:

  • Import and edit videos without internet connection
  • Apply transitions, effects, and filters offline
  • Export videos locally on your device
  • Save projects without network access

Your videos never leave your device. Analytics data is queued locally and transmitted when you have an internet connection.

Children's Privacy

EditorPro does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. The anonymous analytics data we collect cannot identify users and is safe for all ages. Parents and guardians can review the app's permissions in iOS Settings.

In compliance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), we do not collect, use, or disclose personal information from children under 13.

Data Security

Your data security is ensured through our architecture:

  • All video processing happens locally on your device
  • No data transmission means no risk of interception
  • Your videos are protected by iOS's security model
  • Only you have access to your projects and edits

We recommend keeping your device secure with a passcode and ensuring iOS is up to date.

Your Rights and Controls

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access Control - Manage photo library permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy → Photos
  • Data Deletion - Delete all app data by deleting EditorPro from your device
  • Cache Management - Clear cache files through the app's settings (if available)
  • Export Freedom - Your edited videos are saved to your photo library and can be moved/deleted as you wish

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of EditorPro after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
